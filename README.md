# SUGAM 1905 (सुगम 1905) — Uttarakhand CM Helpline & Apuni Sarkar Unified Portal

A modernized, GIGW-compliant citizen portal redesign of Uttarakhand's official **CM Helpline 1905** (`cmhelpline.uk.gov.in`) integrated with **Apuni Sarkar** (`eservices.uk.gov.in`), built for the **PRAYAS 2026 ITDA Hackathon**.

**Team:** Harshit Raj, Vaishnavi Bahuguna, Ashish Rana  
**Institution:** Govt. Institute of Technology, Uttarkashi

## Key Features
- **GIGW Accessibility Bar:** Skip-to-main-content, live High Contrast mode, dynamic font sizing (`A-` / `A` / `A+`), and Citizen/Officer demo login.
- **5-Language Live Translation:** Instant switching across English, हिन्दी (Hindi), गढ़वाली (Garhwali), कुमाऊँनी (Kumaoni), and जौनसारी (Jaunsari).
- **5 Citizen-Intent Pillars (15 Essential Portals):** Live search, pillar filter tabs, and upfront document checklists with explicit **No Affidavit Required** notices.
- **3-Step Grievance Filing & Live Tracker (`localStorage`):** Covers all 13 Uttarakhand districts, generates `UK-1905-2026-XXXX` IDs, and tracks progress across a 4-stage visual timeline (`Registered -> Assigned to Dept -> Field Verification -> Resolved`).
- **Interactive Citizen Tools:** Certificate Verification & Download (`UK-CERT-2026-7741`), Training Manual & CPGRAMS Guide, WhatsApp 1905 QR Connect, and 1–5 Star Citizen Satisfaction Survey.
